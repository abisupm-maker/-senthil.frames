BEHIND EVERY FRAME — A POINT OF VIEW
Photographer · Cinematographer · Creator

Hi, I’m Senthilkumar.

I don’t just capture what happens. I look for what it feels like.

Based in Erode, Tamil Nadu, I’m a visual storyteller drawn to genuine emotion, thoughtful composition, and cinematic light. For 5+ years, I’ve worked across weddings, engagements, events, portraits, and college productions — turning real moments into stories people can return to.

From the quiet details before a ceremony to the energy of a packed celebration, I love being ready for the unscripted frame. My approach blends candid observation, purposeful camera movement, clean editing, and colour that supports the mood instead of overpowering it.
